> Show HN: A curated list of 127 genuinely useful AI apps

## First comment / body (150–250 words)

After months of collecting AI bookmarks that went nowhere, I built a curated list of AI apps that actually hold up.

It's organized into 10 categories — chatbots, coding, search & research, writing, image, video, audio, productivity, automation, and local/open-source tools — with 127 apps total. The rules are simple: every entry needs a working link, an honest one-line description, and must genuinely stand out in its category. If it's hype, abandoned, or just a thin wrapper around another model, it doesn't make the cut.

The list is kept lean on purpose. There are directories with tens of thousands of tools, and most of that is noise. I'd rather someone scan 127 options in ten minutes than drown in 10,000.

I also try to keep it alive rather than letting it rot: links are verified when entries land, and reported dead links get fixed as they come up. It's a living document more than a static page.

If you spot a dead link, a tool that deserves a spot, or a description that's off, I'd genuinely appreciate the feedback — contribution guidelines are in the repo.

Built in the open. Nothing is affiliate-linked and nothing is paid placement, so the ordering reflects what I actually use and recommend.

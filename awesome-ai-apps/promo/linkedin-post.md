# LinkedIn Post — awesome-ai-apps

**Suggested headline:**
"I stopped bookmarking AI tools. I built a list instead."

---

There are thousands of AI apps. Most of them will be forgotten in a year. So instead of bookmarking another 50 links I'd never open, I built the opposite: a curated, deliberately lean list of **127 AI apps that genuinely stand out** — organized into **10 categories**, with **verified links**, updated regularly.

**The problem with "AI directories":**
- They optimize for volume: 5,000 entries, half of them dead links.
- Most entries are wrappers or me-too clones with no reason to exist.
- Bookmarks become graveyards you never return to.

**What we did differently:**
- Only apps that genuinely stand out make the cut. Lean by design.
- Every link is verified — no dead ends, no bait.
- Alphabetical within each category, so scanning is fast and predictable.
- MIT-licensed and open to contributions: spot a great app we missed? Open a PR.

**The 10 categories:**
AI Chatbots, AI Coding, AI Search & Research, AI Writing, AI Image, AI Video, AI Audio & Voice, AI Productivity & Knowledge, AI Automation & Agents, and Local & Open Source.

**A few highlights:**
- **AI Coding:** GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, Devin, Bolt.new, plus open-source agents like Aider and Cline.
- **AI Image:** Midjourney, DALL·E, FLUX, Ideogram, Leonardo AI.
- **AI Video:** Sora, Google Veo, Runway, Kling AI, HeyGen.
- **AI Audio & Voice:** ElevenLabs, Suno, Whisper, Hume AI.
- **AI Productivity:** NotebookLM, Notion AI, Otter.ai, Fathom.
- **Local & Open Source:** Ollama, Open WebUI, OpenHands, Jan.

**Why it matters:**
AI is moving faster than anyone's bookmark system. A short, trustworthy, current list beats a bloated one every time. 127 strong entries — not 1,000 filler ones.

Save it. Share it. And if a tool earned its spot, tell me — the list is meant to be used.

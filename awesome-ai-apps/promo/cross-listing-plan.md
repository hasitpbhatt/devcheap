## Target list (10)

### Tier 1 — Free, GitHub awesome lists (do these first)

**1. awesome-ai — github.com/hades217/awesome-ai**
- **Why:** One of the most-starred general AI awesome lists, maintained since 2017 with an active editor's-picks section.
- **Method:** Fork, add a row to the relevant section (curated lists / directories), open a PR per its CONTRIBUTING.md. Follow the "one line: `Name — short description`" rule.
- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** High — we are exactly an "awesome AI" resource.

**2. ai-collection / thataicollection.com — github.com/ai-collection/ai-collection**
- **Why:** Large open-source collection (1.3M+ monthly visitors to the site); has a dedicated submit form.
- **Method:** Submit via https://www.thataicollection.com/submit/ (app submission form) or open a GitHub issue/PR adding the repo.
- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** High.

**3. awesome (sindresorhus) — github.com/sindresorhus/awesome**
- **Why:** The canonical awesome list; being listed there grants wide discoverability among awesome-list curators.
- **Method:** PR adding our repo under the `Artificial Intelligence` section, following strict awesome-list guidelines (quality, one-line, no self-promo).
- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** Medium-High (competitive, high bar).

### Tier 2 — High-traffic AI directories

**4. There's An AI For That (TAAFT) — theresanaiforthat.com**
- **Why:** ~546K monthly visits, DR 77, dofollow backlinks, intent-driven users.
- **Method:** Submit at https://theresanaiforthat.com/submit/ — create a free account, submit name/URL/tagline/description + logo, wait for moderation (typically 1–7 days). Optional paid featured placement.
- **Cost:** Free (featured is paid). | **Fit:** High.

**5. Futurepedia — futurepedia.io**
- **Why:** 5M+ monthly visitors, DR ~62–72, strong category SEO.
- **Method:** Submit at https://www.futurepedia.io/submit-tool — paid listings only ($247 basic, $497 verified, or $20 lifetime on futurepedia.wiki). Editorial approval applies.
- **Cost:** Paid ($20–$497). | **Fit:** High if budget allows; otherwise skip.

**6. Future Tools — futuretools.io**
- **Why:** ~3M monthly visitors, free submissions, community-voted.
- **Method:** Submit at https://www.futuretools.io/submit-a-tool (form: name, URL, tagline, category, description). Free tier + optional paid promotion.
- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** High.

### Tier 3 — Additional reach (fill out 8–10)

**7. Toolify.ai — toolify.ai**

- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** Medium.

- **Method:** Form submission at https://aitoolarena.tech/submit and https://toolscout.ai/submit (name, URL, category, description, logo).
- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** Medium.

**10. SearchAITools — searchaitool.com**
- **Why:** Free, permanent dofollow listing, human-reviewed within 48h, explicitly no pay-to-win.
- **Method:** Submit via https://searchaitool.com submit flow (link, pricing, short pitch).
- **Cost:** Free. | **Fit:** Medium-High.

## Notes

- **Relevance beats volume.** Our top 3 targets are **awesome-ai**, **There's An AI For That**, and **Future Tools** — all free (or low-cost), high-traffic, and directly on-topic.
- Always adapt the blurb's category/description to each directory's form; never paste one generic profile everywhere.
- Never pay for rankings or "guaranteed placement" services; stick to official submission channels above.
- Keep repo stats fresh: re-verify star/fork counts before each submission.

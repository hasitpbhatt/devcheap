## Recommended repo description (under 120 chars)

> Curated list of the best AI apps and tools, organized into 10 categories. 127 verified, hand-picked picks, updated regularly.

Count: 118 characters.

### Alternatives

- "The best AI apps, ranked & curated. 127 verified AI tools across 10 categories — chatbots, coding, image, video, audio, and more." (125 chars)
- "Hand-picked best AI applications: 127 tools across 10 categories. Updated regularly with verified links, kept lean on quality."

### Notes

- Keep `awesome` and `awesome-list` — they match how users discover similar repos and the classic `awesome-*` convention.
- Swap `ai-coding` for `ai-programming` if analytics show that term outranks; both are queried.
- Add `local-llm` if the Local & Open Source category (9 apps) becomes a growth focus — it targets the self-hosting niche.
- Add `productivity` if you want to attract the knowledge-work audience that lands on AI Productivity & Knowledge.
- Do NOT add brand topics like `chatgpt` alone; it already has high authority sites and adds little long-tail value. Keep it as one topic only.

## Why this setup

- **Under 120 chars** keeps the full description visible in GitHub search and Google results without truncation.
- **10-category framing** differentiates this repo from generic "AI tools" lists and reinforces the 127-app stat.
- **Topics mirror category names** from the README table of contents, aligning repo metadata with on-page content for consistent SEO signals.

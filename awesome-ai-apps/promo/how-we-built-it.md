# How We Built awesome-ai-apps

Most "AI directories" fail because they optimize for volume: 5,000 entries, half of them dead links, one-sentence descriptions that read like they were written by an LLM. Nobody actually uses them.

We wanted the opposite — a small, honest list you can genuinely work from. So we built awesome-ai-apps: a curated collection of **127 AI apps across 10 categories**, with verified links and a rule that is easy to state and hard to enforce: only apps that genuinely stand out get in.

**The curation bar.** Every entry starts as a candidate and has to survive manual review. Does it do something meaningfully better than the alternatives? Is it a real product, not a wrapper? If it's me-too, it's out. Lean is the design: we'd rather have 127 strong entries than 1,000 filler ones. When an app stops being excellent, it gets cut. That "kept lean" promise is the feature, not an accident.

**The structure.** Apps are grouped into 10 categories — AI Chatbots, AI Coding, AI Search & Research, AI Writing, AI Image, AI Video, AI Audio & Voice, AI Productivity & Knowledge, AI Automation & Agents, and Local & Open Source. Within each category, everything is alphabetical, so scanning is fast and predictable. Every link is verified before it ships: broken links are the number-one trust-killer in this space, and we don't ship them.

**Open by default.** The list is MIT-licensed and lives in the open. Anyone can suggest a new app by opening a pull request following the CONTRIBUTING guidelines. The community isn't a nice-to-have — it's the engine. A reader finds an app we missed, a PR lands, and it's live. That's the model: a list maintained by the people who actually use it.

**What stands out.** Several sections could be lists of their own. AI Coding spans GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and Claude Code all the way to open-source agents like Aider, Cline, and Roo Code. AI Video covers Sora, Google Veo, Runway, and Kling AI. Local & Open Source keeps the whole stack honest with Ollama, Open WebUI, and OpenHands. And AI Automation pulls together n8n, Zapier, and Make for the workflow crowd, while AI Productivity anchors everyday tools like NotebookLM and Notion AI.

**What's next.** Keeping a lean list current is a job, not an event. We update it regularly, re-verify links, and re-judge whether each app still earns its place. The goal never changes: 127 apps, 10 categories, zero fluff — a list you'd actually bookmark and use.

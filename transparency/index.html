How a link on this site works

Every outbound link carries a utm_source=devcheap.click tag. When you click it, the vendor sees that the visit came from DevCheap. If you sign up and pay, we get a commission. If you do not sign up, we get nothing.

  • The commission never changes the price you pay.
  • Affiliate status does not change a deal's rating.
  • Affiliate links are marked with a badge on the card and on the detail page.
  • Every deal stays listed whether or not it pays us. {{NON_AFFILIATE_COUNT}} of {{DEAL_COUNT}} deals pay nothing at all.

How we pick partners

A vendor gets an affiliate link only if four things are true.

  • The offer is real. We found it on the vendor's own pricing or program page, not in a press release.
  • The terms are current. The credit amount, free tier limits, and eligibility rules match what the vendor publishes.
  • The deal is useful to a developer without a company. If it needs a signed contract, it gets marked startup-only.
  • The link works from a plain browser. No cookie wall, no interstitial, no forced signup before the pricing page.

We do not accept payment to list a deal. We do not accept payment to change a rating. If a vendor asks for either, we say no and it does not go in the catalog.

What we have not done yet

Being straight about the gaps matters more than the wins. Here is what is missing.

  • We have not published team-test records. The badge and the tracking exist. Until a human has actually signed up and used a product, it stays off. 0 of {{DEAL_COUNT}} deals carry it today.
  • We do not publish a per-deal commission rate. Networks do not give us clean numbers to publish, and guessing would be worse than silence.
  • We do not take sponsorship for ratings or Top Picks slots. A sponsor can buy a category page slot, never a score.
